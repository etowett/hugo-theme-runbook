{{- /* article/prev-next.html — adjacent-post navigation, scoped to the section. `.PrevInSection` / `.NextInSection` rather than the site-wide `.Prev` / `.Next`: site-wide ordering walks straight out of the blog and into the About page. ▸ Hugo's `.Prev` is the previous page IN THE COLLECTION, and the default collection order is date-descending — so `.PrevInSection` is the NEWER post and `.NextInSection` is the OLDER one. That is the opposite of what the words suggest and it is a standing source of themes with their post navigation reversed. The variables below are named for what they actually hold. Renders nothing when the post is alone in its section — a nav landmark containing no links is worse than no landmark. */ -}} {{- $rb := partialCached "utils/settings.html" . -}} {{- if $rb.showPrevNext -}} {{- $newer := .PrevInSection -}} {{- $older := .NextInSection -}} {{- if or $newer $older -}}
{{- with $older }} {{ i18n "previousPost" }} {{ .LinkTitle }} {{- end }} {{- with $newer }} {{ i18n "nextPost" }} {{ .LinkTitle }} {{- end }}
{{- end -}} {{- end -}}
